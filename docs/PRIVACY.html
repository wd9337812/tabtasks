English

TabTasks is a browser side-panel to-do & page-capture extension. We want to be crystal clear: we do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

What data exists, and where does it live?
Everything you create in TabTasks — task titles, page links you capture, page text excerpts you choose to save, list names, tags, and due dates — is stored only in your own browser via the chrome.storage API. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the extension (or clearing your browser data) deletes it permanently. We have no server that holds user data, because there is no user data to hold.

Why does the extension request broad permissions?
TabTasks reads the title and URL of the page you are actively viewing, and (if you are a Pro user) a short text excerpt, only at the moment you click “Capture”. That information is written to your local storage. It is not sent anywhere, not logged, and not retained in any temporary server memory.

Licensing & payments.
Pro purchases are processed by Stripe (stripe.com) on their checkout pages; payment card details are handled entirely by Stripe and never reach us. To activate a Pro license, the extension verifies a license key locally in your browser. We do not receive usage telemetry from the extension.

Privacy policy for the Chrome Web Store listing: this extension practices Limited Use? No — it practices no use: no data leaves the device.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/wd9337812/tabtasks or email the developer via the GitHub profile.

中文

TabTasks 是一款浏览器侧边栏待办与网页收集扩展。我们声明： 本扩展不收集、不传输、不出售、不共享任何个人数据。

数据存在哪里？
你在 TabTasks 中创建的一切内容——任务标题、收集的网页链接、保存的正文摘要、 列表名称、标签与到期日——仅通过 chrome.storage 存储在你自己的 浏览器本地，永不离开你的设备。卸载扩展（或清除浏览器数据）即永久删除。 我们没有存放用户数据的服务器，因为根本没有可存放的用户数据。

为什么申请较宽的权限？
仅在你点击「收集当前页面」的那一刻，扩展会读取当前页面的标题、网址， （Pro 用户）以及一小段正文摘要，并写入你的本地存储。这些信息不会被发送到任何 地方，不会被记录，也不会在任何服务器中留存。

许可与支付。
Pro 买断由 Stripe 在其结账页处理，银行卡信息完全由 Stripe 管理，不经过我们。 激活时扩展在你的浏览器本地校验 License Key。我们不接收任何使用遥测。
